
# E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

Developed an interactive **E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance and generate actionable business insights.

The project focuses on understanding **revenue, orders, product categories, regions, payment methods, discounts, and customer ratings** through interactive visualizations and KPI metrics.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **Microsoft Excel**
* **Power Query**
* **Data Visualization**
* **Data Analysis**

## 📈 Key KPIs

* **Total Orders: ** 5,000
* **Total Quantity Sold: ** 20K+
* **Total Revenue: ** ₹5.11M

## 🔍 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard provides analysis across:

* Revenue by Product Category
* Revenue by Payment Method
* Regional Sales Performance
* Customer Ratings
* Discount Analysis
* Order & Quantity Performance
* Interactive KPI Monitoring

## 💡 Key Insights

* **Electronics** generated the highest revenue among the product categories.
* **Card payments** contributed the highest revenue among the payment methods.
* The **West region** recorded the highest number of orders.
* Sales performance was analyzed across **regions, categories, payment methods, discounts, and customer ratings**.
* Interactive filters and slicers allow users to explore the data from different business perspectives.
